### ⚡ Engineering Focus

* **Production Systems:** GeoAI & Climate VaR haircut engines with Sentinel-2 multi-spectral tensors and InSAR subsidence modeling.
* **Deep Learning & Forecasting:** Multi-horizon financial time-series architectures (PatchTST, LSTM, 1D-CNN) with walk-forward validation.
* **Computer Vision:** Organ-Prompted Swin-TransUNet and Macenko stain normalization for biomedical tissue segmentation.
* **LLM Architectures:** Self-correcting multi-agent loops, lightweight RAG pipelines, and automated policy extraction.
